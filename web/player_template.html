MINER 2049er · TRS-80 MODEL III

A port of Bill Hogue's 1982 Atari game to the Model III's 128 × 48 block graphics, running here in a Z80 emulator. __VERSION__

← → walk   ↑ ↓ climb   Space jump   Esc pause (BREAK). On the title screen a digit picks the starting station (0 = 10); leave it alone and the demonstration runs. Walk over every girder section to claim it before the bonus runs out; picking up an item makes the mutants edible for a few seconds.

Station 3 and 7: stand in a booth and press 1–4 to beam to that booth. Station 8: on the lift, Enter takes the controls and the arrows drive it; Enter or Space lets go. Space still jumps when you are not driving. Station 10: each TNT bundle adds its tons to the charge; walk into the cannon, roll it with the arrows, Space fires. One ton reaches the lowest platform, three the top; four is too many.

Testing keys: I on the title screen turns invincibility on or off (a * at the end of the status line; the screen flashes where he would have died). With it on, during play: hold U to see only the land still to claim, with a count; N goes to the next station.
